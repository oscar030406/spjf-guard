=== G2 LAZINESS ===
the guard fires on q only when over[q] >= B, and over[q] <= In_q always;
so at every firing the constraint In_q <= B is already saturated.  Combined
with the necessity proposition (any policy with excess <= G has In <= kG+(3k-2)L),
no wrapper enforcing a work budget can defer q longer and still promise G.
