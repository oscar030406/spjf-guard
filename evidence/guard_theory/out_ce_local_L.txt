instance: one job of work M at t=0 (rank 0), then 2M-2 unit jobs at t=0,
then 2 unit jobs per tick from t=3M/2 on, so both policies stay saturated.
P defers the big job behind every unit job; FCFS does not.
Every job other than rank 0 has size 1, so for any job i arriving in the
saturated tail, Lam^P_i = Lam^F_i = 1 and the localised bound is (k-1)*2 = 2.

   M     job i   a_i    D=k(W_P-W_F)-(In-Out)   Lam_in_service   D / bound

    20      60     40                   10                1        5.0
    40     120     80                   20                1       10.0
    80     240    160                   40                1       20.0
   160     480    320                   80                1       40.0

D grows linearly in M while every job in service near i has size 1, so no
bound in terms of the sizes local to i's wait can exist.  The big job is
already finished (under both policies) before any of these jobs arrives.
The prefix max Lam_pref = M does bound it, as claim A says.
