R3-1  CRITICAL.  Section 6.4's conjecture is FALSE, and open problem 8.1 is
      settled.  The conjecture says the wrapper's additive constant on the wait
      is "roughly flat in k at about 1.75 L" and that "c(k) = Theta(k) with a
      slope of 2 rather than the proved 3"; open problem 8.1 asks whether In_i
      can be pushed to B + kL on the same instance that saturates Theorem 1.
      It can.  Build the note's OWN Lemma-1'' cascade for m rounds, then at
      T_m inject, in index order, all arriving at T_m:
          k-1 "new bigs" of work L      (ranks below the victim)
          the victim i,   of work L
          one "sync" overtaker of work f = rho_m/(k-1) = L(1-((k-1)/k)^m)
          one "pre"  overtaker of work L
          k "final" overtakers of work L
      with budget B = f + L + 1 and a base policy that dispatches the sync
      overtaker, then the new bigs and the pre overtaker, then the finals, and
      never the victim.  Then: FCFS starts i at T_m with rho^F = (k-1)L; the
      sync overtaker frees all k servers simultaneously at T_m + f; the pre
      overtaker and the new bigs free them all again at T_m + f + L, at which
      instant over_i = f + L = B - 1 < B, so the guard does not fire and the
      base fills all k servers with the finals; they all complete at
      T_m + f + 2L, over_i jumps to B - 1 + kL >= B, the guard fires, and i
      starts with rho^P = 0.  Exactly:
          In_i = B - 1 + kL ,  Out_i = 0 ,  D_i = rho_m + (k-1)L ,
          c = (k*excess - B)/L = ((k-1) f + (2k-1) L - 1)/L  ->  3k - 2.

R3-2  CRITICAL (proof gap; the claim is true).  "Approached, never attained"
      (Lemma 1'') and "the inequality of Theorem 1 is strict" (Theorem 3) are
      NOT proved.  Lemma 1'''s proof ends "Since rho_j < (k-1)L for every
      finite j, and Lemma 1 gives <= (k-1)L, the bound is a supremum that is
      never attained" -- which shows only that THIS FAMILY does not attain it.
      Nothing rules out some other instance attaining (k-1)L, and Theorem 3's
      strictness is derived from that non-attainment.  Exhaustive search does
      not settle it either (max (U_A-U_B)/L found over 4,609,908 ordered
      schedule pairs: 2/3 at k=2,3; max |D|/L over 3,362,238 schedules: 1.5).
      FIX (three lines, please insert):  D = U_A - U_B is continuous (arrivals
      add equally to both) and piecewise linear.  Suppose D(t) = (k-1)L for
      some t and let t1 be the first such time; since D(0) = 0 < (k-1)L, the
      last linear piece before t1 has strictly positive slope, so on it
      min(N_A,k) < min(N_B,k), hence N_A <= k-1 and N_A >= 1, hence all of A's
      jobs are in service and U_A strictly decreases on that piece.  Letting
      u -> t1 from the left, U_A(t1^-) <= (k-1)L and D(t1^-) = (k-1)L force
      U_B(t1^-) = 0 and U_A(t1^-) = (k-1)L; but U_A is strictly decreasing on
      the piece and bounded by (k-1)L on it, so U_A(t1^-) < (k-1)L.
      Contradiction.  Hence |U_A - U_B| < (k-1)L always, so in step (4) of
      Theorem 1, |R^P_i - R^F_i| < (k-1)L strictly, so |D_i| < 2(k-1)L
      strictly, which is exactly what Theorem 3 needs.
