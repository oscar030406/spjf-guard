# Reservation-and-refund guard (rule R): results

Saved verbatim from the final report of that study, 2026-09-23. Scripts and logs in this folder
(`rguard.py`, `run_c1.py`, `run_c1b.py`, `run_c2.py`, `run_c3.py`, `run_c4.py`, `run_c6.py`,
`out_c*.txt`, `README.md`). Not yet independently verified; see `../reservation_guard_verify/`.

**Rule R as tested** — `overR[q](t) = Σ_{j≻q dispatched} (C_j if completed by t else ℓ_j)`; accept the base's proposal `j` iff it is the head, or `overR[q]+ℓ_j ≤ Z_q` for every waiting `q≺j`; else dispatch the head. Every phase dispatch tested separately; same-phase overtakers carry the full reservation. `enum_runs` enumerates all base policies, so "exhaustive" covers policies too.

- **C1 — PROVED.** 321,050 runs / 1,425,796 job checks, 0 rule-R violations (`out_c1.txt`). Rule R: `max(In−B)=0`; rule C: `kL−1`. **Lemma R1:** each job in `In_i` was dispatched at an epoch where the test applied to `i`, and `overR[i]` carries ≥`C_j` for every dispatched job whether completed or not, so `Σ_{u≤s}C_{j_u} ≤ overR[i](t_s)+ℓ_{j_s} ≤ Z_i`; hence `In_i ≤ Z_i`. **Theorem R2:** with `Z≡B`, Cor. "sufficient" gives `excess ≤ B/k+(2−2/k)L`, instance form `k·excess ≤ B+Λ_{k−1}(a_i)+Λ^{<i}_{k−1}` (T3-2), and at k=1 `excess ≤ B` exactly. The `kL` overshoot of eq. (guardin) disappears; `(2−2/k)L` survives and is still approached — `out_c1b.txt` runs the S8.2 witness at `B=f+L+1`, `In_R=B` exactly, ratio → 2k−2 (1.875/3.59/5.05 at k=2/3/4). A closed-form witness attaining it is OPEN. Risk: caps must be *enforced*, not predicted.
- **C2 — PROVED at k=1.** 468 family rows, 0 mismatches; 117,108 relabelling tests, 0 failures (`out_c2.txt`). **Prop. R4:** closure is exactly `min(1, max(0,⌊(G−ℓ)/σ⌋+1)/n)` — `⌊G/σ⌋/n` at `ℓ=C` (Prop. A1's size-aware optimum) and `(⌊(G−L)/σ⌋+1)/n` at `ℓ=L` (Cor. k1_frontier); before the s-th short the head has `overR=(s−1)σ`, so the test is `(s−1)σ+ℓ≤G`. **Theorem R3 (pointwise maximality, k=1):** if `overR[h]+ℓ_j>G` at the head `h`, relabel every dispatched-unfinished job (incl. `j`) to its cap — observables and the opaque base transcript are unchanged, `Out_h=0`, so `excess[h]=In_h ≥ overR[h]+ℓ_j>G`, and `W_FCFS[h]` is untouched since all relabelled jobs rank above `h`. So `Z=G` is the largest safe allowance, vs `B*=G−L+1` for completed-work charging, **and it needs no integer lattice** — this removes the real-valued defect flagged in verification.md §5. k≥2 OPEN.
- **C3 — PROVED (In part).** `out_c3.txt`, L=12, B=10, k=1/2/4: rule R's `In = ℓ⌊B/ℓ⌋ ≤ B` in all 18 cells and in 7,200 random instances; rule C reaches `B+kℓ`, `=kL=48` at k=4, ℓ=L. An admitted overtaker always has `ℓ_j ≤ Z_q`, so the entry threshold is `G ≥ L` at k=1 (meeting Prop. floor with equality) and `(2−1/k)L` at ℓ=L, against `(3−2/k)L` for Algorithm 1 — **the threshold drops by exactly L**.
- **C4 — (a) PROVED, (b) OPEN.** `out_c4.txt`. **Prop. R5:** after m cascade rounds FCFS is empty at `T_m` while P holds k−1 jobs with remaining `f`; inject `g` (lower rank) then the victim — `excess = f → L` with `In_i=Out_i=0`. This is the sharpest form of the Ω(L) floor: *no* charging rule can see it, which is the honest defence of the residual `(2−2/k)L`. (b) measured law `max_t(U_P−U_FCFS) ≤ (k−1)·min(L,B)` survived 375,894 exhaustive runs + 4,000 random, 0 violations; if proved it sharpens R2 to `B+(k−1)min(L,B)+(k−1)L`.
- **C5 — novel, with one gap.** Opened Mu'alem–Feitelson TPDS 2001: conservative backfilling moves a job ahead "only if they do not delay any job in the queue"; with one server-slot per job the head's reservation *is* the next free server, so any overtake delays it — **it collapses to FCFS here**, and rule R is its budgeted generalisation. Opened arXiv:1905.03439v1 Def. 1: guardrails charge *dispatched* work per size-rank `r=⌊log_c x⌋` per server and constrain `|G^r_s−G^r_{s'}| ≤ g·c^{r+1}` — spatial balance across servers, size known at dispatch, no reference policy, no refund. Nudge charges a count, at arrival, in steady state. **Lindsay et al. 2013 (10.1002/cpe.2860) could not be opened** (Wiley 403, Springer auth wall) — do not write a novelty sentence until someone reads it.
- **C6 — k=1 closed, k=2 OPEN.** 2,049,624 runs (`out_c6.txt`). k=1: `E(B)=B+L−1` for rule C (so `B*=G−L+1`, matching Thm. k1_maximal) and `E(B)=B` for rule R (so `B*=G`, matching R2 exactly). k=2: this family still tolerates `B=5,6,8` at `G=3,4,5` against proved `0,2,4`. Note rule R's k=1 budget `G` **exceeds** the quoted adversary bound `k(G−L)`; that bound is specific to completed-work charging and must not be quoted against a reserving rule.

**Do not claim dominance:** at `ℓ=L` rule R *matches*, not beats, the sharp completed-work guard on the two-size family at equal promise. The gain is the certificate, the lower entry threshold, and the cap-parameterised k=1 maximality.
